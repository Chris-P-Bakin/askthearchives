# NACA OCR campaign: results

*Living document. The narrative is revised as batches land; the ledger table at the bottom is
regenerated by `pipeline/ledger.py --batch N --write` and should not be edited by hand.*

## What this is

A campaign to turn the NASA NACA technical report corpus (15,933 reports on NTRS) into
page-level text that is searchable, citable and publicly redistributable. Every page keeps
its original image, a tesseract word-box layer, and the best available transcript with its
provenance (which model, which configuration hash). Batches are published as immutable
torrents with errata overlays and a per-batch index shard; the ledger below says what each
batch cost and how reliable it is.

## Method

1. **Ingest** (pass1): download the NTRS PDF, rasterize and deskew pages, run tesseract.
2. **Gate v1**: a page escalates to a vision model if tesseract mean confidence is below 70 or
   figures cover more than 20% of the page. The rest keep their tesseract text.
3. **Campaign transcription**: gated pages go out as BOINC work units to a vision-language
   model, Qwen3-VL-8B with the v2.1 LoRA (config hash `6ecf7d0dbeb2`), with a loop guard
   (compression-ratio detector plus a temperature-0.4 retry).
4. **Escalation lanes**: pages the campaign model cannot be trusted on are re-run on a larger
   model. Vision-failed and empty pages go to the 27B (`6a169a212aac`); digit-dense table
   pages that fail the table consistency checks go to the 235B (OpenRouter, capped at $120).
   Pages still unresolved land in a review queue and are published flagged, not hidden.

## Quality gates

Engines are accepted only after passing a ground-truth gate: median character error rate at
or below 0.1032 over 30 hand-checked pages. Results so far:

| Engine | Hash | Median CER |
|---|---|---|
| 8B + LoRA v2.1 (campaign) | `6ecf7d0dbeb2` | 0.0940 |
| 27B (escalation) | `6a169a212aac` | 0.0975 |

Both pass on the median only. The error distribution has a heavy tail (the 8B mean CER is
0.235; the 27B p75 is about 0.33), so the median should not be read as a typical page error.
Table pages are the known weak spot, and CER agreement between engines cannot see wrong digits,
which is why table pages are flagged by a separate consistency verifier.

## Batch 0

500 documents (1915 to 1923), 10,435 pages. Gate v1 sends 4,963 pages (47.6%) to the vision
model, 4,955 of them as work units. A 10-unit BOINC alpha ran end to end on one RTX 3090
(10 valid, 0 invalid, 10 assimilated; 3 table pages queued for flags; about 19 s per page at two
concurrent streams). Page images stage to R2.

## Compute lanes

- **BOINC plane**: the work generator, validator and assimilator feed a tailnet-only alpha
  project; workers are llama-server containers on local GPUs.
- **27B lane**: runs in a GPU window with the campaign worker stopped; drained 5 of 5
  vision-failed pages live.
- **235B lane**: paid, key supplied by the operator at run time, pilot first.

## Caveats

- Spend counts roughly $5.72 of trial-era 235B runs against the $120 cap, which is
  conservative.
- Throughput (pages/hour) in the ledger is measured from assimilation timestamps and is only
  meaningful once a batch has many assimilated pages.
- The ledger's `warnings` count is nonzero whenever the arithmetic does not reconcile
  (gated + ungated = OCR pages; escalated pages within gated) or BOINC counts are
  unavailable. Treat a warning as a prompt to investigate, not a result.
